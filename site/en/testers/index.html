We're testing Cat Bubble Island on Google Play before launch. We're looking for people with an Android phone who will play a little over two weeks and tell us what they think: where they got stuck, what felt boring or what broke.

What you need

  • An Android phone.
  • The Google account you use for the Play Store on that phone. Use the same account in all three steps.
  • A few minutes on a few days, over 14 days.

How to join

  1. Join the testers group

    Sign in with your Google account and tap "Join group". That makes you eligible for the test.

    Join the group

    Sign-ups open very soon. In the meantime, email us and we'll add you by hand: {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}. Include the Gmail address you use for the Play Store.

  2. Accept the invitation

    Open this link with the same account and tap "Become a tester". The page confirms that you're a tester.

    Accept the invitation

    If it says the app isn't available, wait a few minutes after step 1 and try again.

  3. Download the game

    On your phone, open the Google Play listing and install Cat Bubble Island.

    Get it on Google Play

During the test

  • Google requires the test to run for at least 14 days. Please stay in the program until it ends: every tester counts.
  • Play a little on a few days. Tell us what you think from the game (Settings → Support) or at {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}.
  • To keep your progress safe, link Google Play Games in Settings → View profile → Save progress.

Frequently asked questions

Does it cost anything?

Downloading and playing is free. In-app purchases are optional and not needed to take part. If you buy something, Google Play charges it like in any other game.

I accepted the invitation but can't install the game

Check that the Play Store is using the same Google account you used to join the group and accept the invitation. If you have several accounts on your phone, switch accounts from your profile picture in the Play Store.

What data do you see?

When you join the group, we see your email address. We use it only to run the test and reply to you. The game handles your data as described in the Privacy Policy.

How do I leave the test?

On the step 2 page, tap "Leave the program" and leave the Google group. You can uninstall the game at any time. To delete your game data: Settings → Privacy and legal → Delete account and data, or request it on the web.

We will never ask for passwords, verification codes or card details to take part.
