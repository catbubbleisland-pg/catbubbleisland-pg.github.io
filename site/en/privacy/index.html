1. Who is responsible

Cat Bubble Island (the “game”) is published by {{PUBLISHER_NAME}} ({{PUBLISHER_COUNTRY_EN}}), which is responsible for the personal data described in this policy. Contact: {{PRIVACY_EMAIL}}.

This policy applies to the Android version of the game distributed through Google Play and to this website.

2. Summary

  • We use a game account and your progress so you can play and keep your progress.
  • Analytics, crash reports and rewarded ads are optional. They stay off until you allow them, and you can change your choices at any time in Settings → Privacy and legal.
  • Ads in this version are rewarded only, you choose whether to watch each one, and they are not personalized.
  • Purchases are processed by Google Play. We never receive your card details.
  • You can delete your account and data in the game (Settings → Privacy and legal → Delete account and data) or request it on the web.
  • We do not sell your personal data.

4. Your choices

When you first play, the game asks about the optional uses. Each one starts off and stays off until you turn it on. You can review or change your choices at any time in Settings → Privacy and legal. Declining does not affect saving your progress, and you can play the whole game with every optional choice off.

Turning an option off stops new data from being sent from that moment. It does not by itself delete data already sent; to remove it, see section 11.

5. Rewarded ads

This version only shows rewarded ads: short ads that you choose to watch in exchange for a bonus (for example, extra shots or doubled coins). There are no banner or interstitial ads. Ads are served through Unity LevelPlay and Unity Ads and are requested as non-personalized in every country, which means they are not targeted using a profile of your activity. The ad providers still process the data listed in section 3 to deliver and measure ads, cap their frequency and prevent fraud.

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), the game shows a privacy message from Google’s User Messaging Platform before ads can be requested. If you do not allow ads, rewarded ads are not available and the rest of the game keeps working.

6. Purchases

In-app purchases are optional and are processed by Google Play under Google’s terms and privacy policy. We receive the information needed to deliver your purchase (such as the product and the order and transaction identifiers) but never your payment card details. Refunds are handled by Google Play.

7. Support messages

If you write to us, we use your email address and message only to answer you and to handle your request. Please do not send passwords, verification codes, full receipts or payment card numbers.

8. What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal data or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.
  • We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera.
  • This version does not send push notifications and does not use Google Analytics for Firebase.
  • There is no chat and no way for players to contact each other in the game.

9. Service providers and international transfers

We rely on these providers, which process data on our behalf or under their own terms as described above:

  • Unity Technologies — Unity Gaming Services (Authentication, Cloud Save, Remote Config, Analytics), Unity IAP, Unity LevelPlay and Unity Ads. Unity Privacy Policy · Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.
  • Google — Google Play, Google Play Billing, Google Play Games services, Firebase Crashlytics and the User Messaging Platform. Google Privacy Policy · Privacy and Security in Firebase.

These providers may process data in countries other than yours, including the United States. Where required, transfers rely on the safeguards offered by each provider.

10. Retention

  • Game account and progress: kept while your account exists. They are deleted when you delete your account.
  • Analytics: kept according to the retention settings of Unity Analytics and deleted for your account when you request deletion.
  • Crash reports: kept by Firebase Crashlytics according to its retention policy (Firebase documents 90 days).
  • Purchases: delivery records are kept with your game account. Google Play keeps its own order records under Google’s policies. We keep a purchase record after deletion only if the law requires it, and only for the required period.
  • Support messages: deleted {{SUPPORT_RETENTION_DAYS}} days after your request is resolved, unless the request is still open.
  • Deletion requests: we keep only the request reference, dates and status for {{CASE_RECORD_RETENTION_DAYS}} days after it is closed, to show that it was handled.

11. Deleting your account and data

You can delete your game account and its associated data at any time:

  • In the game: Settings → Privacy and legal → Delete account and data.
  • On the web, without the game installed: follow the steps on the account deletion page. We complete web requests within {{DELETION_COMPLETE_DAYS}} days after confirming the account is yours.

Deleting your game account removes your progress, coins, items and companions and cannot be undone. It does not delete your Google account and does not request a refund. Details of what is deleted and what is kept are on the account deletion page.

12. Security

Data sent by the game to our providers travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Access to the services we manage is restricted to the people who need it to run the game. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your data.

13. Age of players

Cat Bubble Island is designed for teens and adults (13 and older) and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used the game and shared personal data with us, contact us and we will delete it.

14. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal data, to object to or restrict some uses, and to withdraw consent at any time without affecting earlier processing. To exercise them, use the in-game options or write to {{PRIVACY_EMAIL}}. We may need to confirm that the account is yours. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

15. Changes

If we change how we use data, we will update this page and its effective date and, when required, ask for your choices again in the game.
