Progress and accounts

How do I keep my progress if I change or reset my phone?

The game creates a game account automatically. To recover it on another phone, link it first: go to Settings → View Profile → Save Progress and sign in with Google Play Games. On the new phone, sign in with the same Google Play Games account. A guest account that was never linked cannot be recovered after uninstalling the game or clearing its data.

Where do I find my Support ID?

Go to Settings → View Profile. Your Support ID appears there; tap Copy and paste it in your email. It helps us find your account. It is not a password and it does not give access to your account.

My progress looks different than expected

Make sure you have an internet connection and that you are signed in with the same Google Play Games account you used before. If the game shows a choice between two saved games, read both options carefully before choosing. If something is still wrong, write to us with your Support ID and what you expected to see. Please do not uninstall the game or clear its data while we investigate.

Purchases

I bought something but did not receive it

Close and reopen the game with an internet connection, using the same Google account you used to pay. Pending purchases are delivered when the game confirms them with Google Play. If it still does not arrive, write to us with your Support ID, the product name and the order number from your Google Play receipt email (it starts with GPA.). Never send your card number.

How do I ask for a refund?

Purchases are processed by Google Play, which handles refunds under its policies: request a refund on Google Play. Deleting your game account does not request a refund.

Rewarded ads

I watched an ad and did not get the reward

The reward is given only when the ad finishes and the ad service confirms it. If the ad was closed early, failed to load or your connection dropped, no reward is given and you can try again later. Ads are optional: you can always keep playing without them.

Why can’t I see the option to watch ads?

The option appears when an ad is available and you are online. In some countries you first need to answer a Google privacy message; you can review it in Settings → Privacy and legal → Advertising privacy options.

Privacy and your data

  • Change analytics and crash report choices at any time in Settings → Privacy and legal.
  • Read our Privacy Policy.
  • Delete your account and data in the game (Settings → Privacy and legal → Delete account and data) or request it on the web.
