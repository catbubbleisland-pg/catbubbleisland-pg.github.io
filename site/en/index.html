Aim, bounce and pop bubbles with Pompon the cat.

Cat Bubble Island is a story-driven bubble shooter in a soft watercolor style. Help Pompon break the seals that trap the colors of four islands, rescue new companions and face the guardians waiting at the end of the road.

Available for Android on Google Play

Coming soon to Google Play for Android phones

How to play

Aim and bounce

Drag to aim and bank your shots off the walls to reach the bubbles that matter.

Match colors

Connect three or more bubbles of the same color to pop them and drop everything hanging below.

Clear the board

Every level has a limited number of shots. Clear the board to win and earn up to three stars.

54 levels across 4 islands

Island 1 · The Garden

12 levels

A quiet garden sealed by mysterious bindings. Vines, wicker cages, rocks and blossoms stand in your way.

Island 2 · A bay of tides

12 levels

Tides shift the board while the friends search the harbor for someone who needs a home.

Island 3 · A lighthouse gone dark

12 levels

Sea fog hides the board as the team tries to bring light back to the lighthouse.

Island 4 · The Desert of the Sleepers

18 levels

Linked seals guard an old archive buried in the sand, and someone is sleeping inside.

Rescue new companions

Choose one companion before each level. Each cat has an active skill and a passive bonus.

Also in the game

  • Illustrated story scenes before and after key levels.
  • Boss battles with several phases.
  • Boosters such as the Rainbow bubble and Color Swap.
  • Link Google Play Games to save your progress to your game account.
  • Play in English or Spanish.

Free to play

Cat Bubble Island is free to download. It offers optional in-app purchases of coins and a starter pack through Google Play, and optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch in exchange for a bonus. You can keep playing without buying anything or watching ads.
