Delete your account and data

Cat Bubble Island · published by {{PUBLISHER_NAME}}

Leer en español

You can ask us to delete your Cat Bubble Island game account and its associated data at any time. You do not need a website account, and you do not need to have the game installed.

Deletion is permanent. You will lose your levels, stars, coins, boosters, companions and any purchases delivered to that account. Deleting your game account does not delete your Google account and does not request a refund (refunds are handled by Google Play).

Option 1: in the game (fastest)

  1. Open Cat Bubble Island and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Delete account and data.
  3. Read the summary and confirm. You need an internet connection; the game tells you if the request could not be sent.

This deletes your game account and its saved progress from our game services and clears the game data for that account on the device.

Option 2: on the web, without the game

  1. Send an email to {{PRIVACY_EMAIL}} with the subject Account deletion request - Cat Bubble Island. Write the request email
  2. Include, if you have it, your Support ID (Settings → View Profile → Support ID → Copy), whether you linked Google Play Games, and roughly when and on which phone you last played. This helps us find the right account.
  3. We reply within {{DELETION_ACK_DAYS}} days to confirm we received your request and, if needed, to check that the account is yours. A Support ID alone does not prove ownership, so we may ask you for details that only the account holder can see in the game.
  4. After confirming, a member of our team deletes the account in our game services and emails you when it is done, within {{DELETION_COMPLETE_DAYS}} days.

We will never ask for your Google password, verification codes, identity documents or payment card number. If we cannot confirm that the account is yours, we will not delete it and will explain why.
